1. Governance Index (/governance/README.md)
Purpose

This directory defines the governance boundaries, scope, and operating assumptions of the eutube project.

Governance is treated as a first-class system component, not an afterthought.

Documents

Jurisdiction and Geographic Scope
Defines initial operational boundaries and legal scope.

Threat Model
Identifies structural risks, attack surfaces, and mitigation priorities.

Ethics and Accountability (planned)
Will define auditability, transparency obligations, and user rights.

Governance Principles

Transparency over opacity

Explicit scope over silent expansion

Auditability over trust-by-assertion

User agency over algorithmic coercion

Governance documents are versioned, public, and subject to revision with justification.
