One-Paragraph Funder / Partner Introduction

eutube is an early-stage proposal for a bot-resistant, privacy-preserving European media infrastructure designed to reduce automated manipulation and increase transparency in digital communication. Rather than focusing on content moderation alone, the project addresses structural weaknesses in existing platforms through human-first participation models, explicit AI labelling, jurisdictionally coherent deployment, and governance treated as core infrastructure. The project is currently in a public definition and validation phase and is seeking partners and pilot funding aligned with democratic resilience, trustworthy AI, and European digital sovereignty objectives.
