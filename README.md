# eutube

## Overview

**eutube** is a proposal and early-stage implementation of a **bot-resistant, privacy-preserving European media infrastructure**.

It is not a clone of existing US platforms. It is an alternative architecture focused on:

* democratic resilience
* human-first participation
* transparent AI use
* resistance to automated manipulation

This repository exists to:

* make the concept public and timestamped
* document the technical approach
* provide a foundation for collaboration and funding discussions

---

## Problem Statement

Current large-scale media platforms suffer from structural failures:

* bot amplification distorts public discourse
* opaque algorithms reward outrage over accuracy
* foreign-state and commercial interference is difficult to detect
* users lack agency over AI-generated content

These are not moderation problems. They are **architecture problems**.

---

## Core Principles

1. **Human-first participation**
   Verified human interaction is prioritised over engagement metrics.

2. **Bot resistance by design**
   Detection focuses on behavioural patterns, not superficial CAPTCHA-style checks.

3. **AI transparency**
   AI-generated or AI-modified content is explicitly marked and user-filterable.

4. **User agency**
   Users choose whether to view, block, or contextualise flagged content.

5. **Privacy by default**
   Minimal data retention, no behavioural profiling for advertising.

---

## Planned Components

* **Identity & Verification Layer**
  Privacy-preserving human verification mechanisms.

* **Content Pipeline**
  Upload, processing, AI-labelling, and distribution flow.

* **AI Moderation Layer**
  Detection of automated behaviour, synthetic media, and coordinated manipulation.

* **User Controls**
  Explicit toggles for AI-generated content and flagged material.

* **Governance Model**
  Transparent rules, auditability, and public-interest oversight.

---

## Governance Direction

The project is designed to operate as:

* non-extractive infrastructure
* aligned with European digital sovereignty goals
* compatible with public or cooperative ownership models

No advertising-driven engagement optimisation is planned.

---

## Why Europe

* Strong privacy and data protection norms
* Existing regulatory framework for digital rights
* Public funding mechanisms for democratic infrastructure

The platform is intended to be **jurisdictionally and culturally European** by design.
